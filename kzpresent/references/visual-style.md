# KZPresent visual style

## Brand assets and structural references

- Use `../assets/logo.jpg` as the visible «КУЗБАСС-ЦОТ / ПОМНИ О ЖИЗНИ» corporate logo.
- Use `../assets/icon.png` only as a compact UI or decorative brand mark when the full logo is already visible.
- Inspect `../assets/references/*.png` for information hierarchy and page structure only. Do not reproduce their blue palette, logo, or SnowGood identity.

## Canvas and grid

- Use portrait A4: 210 × 297 mm.
- Keep a full-width dark-green header occupying roughly 9–12% of page height.
- Place the full corporate logo at the upper left on a white inset or white clear-space area. Align the title and subtitle to its right.
- Place the date inside the header, preferably right-aligned or at the end of the subtitle line. Keep it clearly visible, on one line, and separated from the title.
- Use consistent outer margins, approximately 5–7% of page width.
- Organize the body into two to four horizontal bands and align edges across bands.
- Fill the page while preserving visible gaps between sections. Avoid tiny orphaned elements.

## Green palette

Sample the logo when exact matching matters. Use these starting values:

| Role | Approximate color |
|---|---|
| Header dark green | `#006B3C` |
| Corporate green | `#009846` |
| Medium green | `#2E8B57` |
| Accent lime | `#72BF44` |
| Pale mint | `#DDF3E5` |
| Page background | `#F3FAF5` |
| Primary text | `#17382A` |
| Secondary text | `#5F7D6C` |
| Warning amber | `#D88900` |

Use white title text on the dark-green header. Use corporate green for section headings, rules, arrows, and key metrics. Use amber only for warnings, failed thresholds, or a losing option.

## Typography

- Use a clean sans-serif with Cyrillic support. Prefer Arial or Aptos when no house font is supplied.
- Use bold or semibold for titles, section headings, metrics, table headers, and conclusions.
- Keep the main title to one or two lines. Never shrink it into body-text scale.
- Keep every visible text element at 12 pt or larger. Prefer 14 pt or larger for body copy, captions, notes, and table cells; use 12 pt only when content cannot be shortened without losing necessary meaning.
- Align numeric comparisons and retain nonbreaking spaces between values and units when practical.

## Components

- Use rounded rectangles with a thin corporate-green outline for light panels.
- Use solid dark-green panels for goals, key results, and contrast blocks.
- Use pale-mint panels for conclusions and recommendations.
- Use simple green arrows in process flows and keep connectors behind nodes.
- Crop technical images cleanly and label each image immediately below it.
- Keep tables flat: dark-green header, light body, subtle rules, selective mint highlighting.
- Avoid gradients, drop shadows, glass effects, decorative patterns, and UI-like buttons.

## Logo integrity

Do not recolor, stretch, crop, redraw, or place text over `../assets/logo.jpg`. Preserve its aspect ratio, white background, border, wording, and clear space.
