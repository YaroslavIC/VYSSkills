# Content patterns

Choose the smallest pattern that supports the intended conclusion.

## Comparison / selection

1. Header: comparison subject and test conditions.
2. Visual row: tested options, setup, or one diagnostic plot.
3. Score or headline metric per option.
4. Comparison table containing only decision-relevant measures.
5. Recommendation panel naming the preferred option and the reason.
6. Conditions, formula, or limitations in small text at the bottom.

## Method / accuracy

1. Header: measured quantity and setup.
2. Goal panel.
3. Short numbered method plus formula or assumption panel.
4. Two or three evidence images with captions.
5. Experimental result and forecast table.
6. Bottom conclusion distinguishing observed from projected accuracy.

## Proposed architecture / next stage

1. Header: proposal name and one-line mechanism.
2. Goal panel.
3. Left-to-right architecture flow with three to five nodes.
4. Mathematical or physical basis.
5. Expected result panel explicitly labeled as an estimate.
6. Numbered implementation plan.
7. Final thesis and validation requirement.

## Copy budget

- Title: up to 12–16 words.
- Header subtitle: one line.
- Goal: one or two sentences.
- Section heading: two to five words.
- Diagram node: up to three short lines.
- Image caption: one line.
- Final conclusion: preferably 25–45 words.
- Footnote or caveat: one or two compact lines.

If content exceeds the page, shorten prose, remove secondary evidence, or ask for a second page. Do not solve overflow by reducing text below 12 pt; prefer 14 pt or larger throughout.
